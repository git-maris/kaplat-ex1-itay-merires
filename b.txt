Barry Allen stared at the loading bar on his screen. "Iris, this `git push` is taking forever. The latency is killing me."
"It's only been two seconds, Barry," she replied.
"Exactly. Too slow." 
A streak of yellow lightning flashed through the room. Barry blurred across the continent, entered the GitHub data center in Ohio, manually toggled the bits on the physical server drives with his vibrating fingertips, and was back in his chair before the loading bar hit **1%**.
"Done," Barry smiled, taking a bite of his pizza. 
Back in Malibu, JARVIS alerted Tony Stark: "Sir, someone just performed a manual commit at **299,792,458** meters per second. I believe the Flash just outran the internet."
