Peter Parker was busy doing his homework when Miles Morales burst into his room, looking frantic.
"Peter, someone's attackin' GitHub headquarters! They're tryin' to delete the world's code!"
Peter's spider-sense was already screaming. He didn't have a second to lose.
He put on his mask and the two heroes swung into the Manhattan night, heading straight for the glowing GitHub building.
Inside the server room, the villain Electro was using his electrical power to fry the mainframes. 
"I'm gonna short-circuit the entire internet!" he laughed as blue sparks flew.
"Hey, Electro! Have you ever heard of a Merge Conflict?" Peter joked as he dodged a high-voltage attack.
While Peter distracted Electro with witty comebacks and agile flips, Miles used his unique stealth power to become invisible.
He crept up behind Electro and unleashed his patented "Venom Blast" on the villain's power core. 
Electro groaned as the energy was drained from his body, and Peter quickly used a high-strength web-ball to wrap him in a conductive cocoon, shorting him out completely.
As the police arrived to take Electro away, Miles looked at the server monitors. A small green checkmark appeared: "Build Successful."
"Nice work, Miles," Peter said, giving him a fist bump. "Teamwork makes the dream work, especially when it comes to saving the world's repositories."
"For real, man," Miles grinned. 
"The internet is safe... for now." The two Spider-Men shot their webs and disappeared into the night, leaving the GitHub staff to finish their code reviews.
